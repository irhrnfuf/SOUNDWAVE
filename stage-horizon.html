Origins & Architectural Evolution

Founded in June 2014: Horizon Arena began as a temporary 12,000-person wooden pier stage on the south bayfront when The Velvet Suns played the inaugural sunset slot.

2019 & 2024 Expansions: Re-engineered into a permanent 110-foot steel proscenium arch with dual catwalks, welcoming over 1.42 million cumulative music fans across 12 festival editions.
