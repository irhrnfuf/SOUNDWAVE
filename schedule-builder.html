Camping, Glamping & Overnight Village Guide

Everything you need to know about sleeping under the Miami stars at Waterfront Park

Camping Options

  • Standard Car Camping: 20x20ft plot for 1 car and up to 2 tents ($45 add-on).
  • Lakeside Glamping Safari: Pre-pitched canvas tent with memory foam bed and AC.
  • RV Oasis: 30-amp and 50-amp power hookups with water refilling.

Campground Amenities

  1. Free filtered drinking water stations across all camp hubs.
  2. Solar-heated showers, grooming stations and phone-charging stations.
  3. General Camp Store stocking ice, sunscreen, snacks and camping gear.

Campground Hours & Access

Thursday Check-In:
Camp gates open Thursday at 2:00 PM for early bird campers.
Quiet Hours:
2:00 AM – 7:30 AM daily in designated quiet family zones.
Monday Check-Out:
All campgrounds close Monday at 12:00 PM noon.
