Origins & Live Record Pressing Tradition

Founded in May 2015: Created in SoundWave's second year as an intimate shaded sanctuary under the North Botanical Banyan & Oak trees, giving indie-folk and orchestral acts natural room resonance.

Live-to-Acetate Lathe (Since 2018): Every headliner set at Vinyl Lawn is cut live onto master lacquer discs onstage and pressed into numbered collector vinyl records for attendees before the weekend ends.
