Why the 2026 Roster Is Our Most Unforgettable Lineup Yet

For three electric nights along Biscayne Bay, SoundWave 2026 brings together stadium-shaking synth pioneers, raw waterfront indie bands, and underground late-night selectors. Every headliner set is a custom-commissioned 90-minute production built exclusively for our 80,000W H2O Soundwall—meaning you won’t see these collaborations, live string sections, or analog visual rigs anywhere else on tour.

Friday 12/18

Sunset Ignition on the Bay

Kick off the weekend as golden hour hits the water with Glass Harbor and The Midnight Chords, leading into a massive opening-night laser & modular synth takeover by Neon Pulse.

♫ Don’t miss: Live Sunset Strings at 6:00 PM
Saturday 12/19

Peak-Hour Electric Fever

Our heaviest night of the weekend. Aurora Echoes teams up with a 12-piece acoustic ensemble before Static Velocity and Acid Cathedral turn the 360° Analog Dome into an all-night warehouse club.

⚡ Don’t miss: 360° Dome B2B Set at 11:00 PM
Sunday 12/20

The Moonlight All-Star Finale

Close out 2026 under the Miami stars as DJ Solaris and Bassline Syndicate shake the pier, culminating in our famous All-Star Finale where every weekend artist joins the main stage together.

★ Don’t miss: Midnight Fireworks & Encore Jam
