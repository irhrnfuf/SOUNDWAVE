Origins & Geodesic Engineering

Founded in October 2016: Analog Dome launched during SoundWave's 3rd year as an experimental 4,800-capacity parachute tent dedicated strictly to live hardware synthesizers, drum machines, and tape loops.

2021 Geodesic Upgrade: Rebuilt as a 95-foot aluminum geodesic lattice lined with 360° projection mapping panels and a Boiler-Room style Platinum backstage cage right behind the DJs.
